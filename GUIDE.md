# GX Optimizer guide

## Install
1. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
2. Click **Load Temporary Add-on** and choose `manifest.json` (or the zip).
3. Open a new tab. The dock sits at the bottom.

## The dock
- Hover to magnify. Click an icon to launch it (it bounces first).
- Drag icons to rearrange. Right-click for Add to locker / Edit / Remove.
- **+** adds a shortcut. When adding or editing you can upload your own icon (PNG, JPG
  or SVG, cropped to a square). Choose Edit later to change it.
- Starters: Steam, YouTube, Roblox and Discord.
- Ctrl-click or middle-click opens in a normal new tab.
- Paste a link anywhere on the page (Ctrl+V) to open it.

## Lockers (your desk)
The droplet button opens your lockers. A locker is a named list of links, like a desk
for one kind of work (School, Gaming, Projects).
- Click a link to open it in a new tab. **Open all** opens the whole locker.
- Add links three ways: paste a link and press Enter, click a dock icon under
  "Add from your dock", or press **Save open tabs** to store this window's tabs.
- **+** makes a new locker. Rename or delete from the buttons at the bottom.
- The toolbar button lists your lockers too, so you can open one without a new tab.

## Cava visualizer
- Click the bars button in the dock to turn it on.
- It reacts to **audio playing in your Firefox tabs** (YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch
  and similar). It does not use your microphone.
- Start music in another tab, then switch to a new tab to watch the bars.
- It cannot hear other apps (Spotify desktop, games, system sounds).
- Sites that protect their audio with DRM (Spotify web, Netflix) may show flat bars.
- A small script runs in your pages for this. It stays idle unless Cava is on and only
  sends 72 numbers per frame to the extension. Nothing leaves your computer.

## Themes
- Built-in: **Cyan**, **Red** and **Green**. Each changes the wallpaper, the dock
  colours, the Cava bars and (optionally) Firefox itself.
- Click the **paint button** to open the theme menu: pick a theme, make a **New theme**
  from one accent colour, **Change wallpaper**, **Reset wallpaper**, edit or delete
  your own themes.
- The toolbar popup also has a theme picker and a "Colour Firefox too" checkbox.

## Animated wallpapers
- In the paint menu choose **Change wallpaper...** and pick a picture, a **GIF**, or an
  **MP4/WebM** video (up to 40 MB).
- Videos play muted and loop, and pause when the tab is hidden to save power.
- MP4 playback depends on the codecs your Firefox has. If an MP4 stays black, convert
  it to WebM.

## Keyboard sound
- Paint menu, then **Keyboard sound...**. Choose **Thock** (deep, ASMR), **Clicky**
  (sharp, gamer) or **My own sound files**, and set the volume.
- It plays when you type on the new tab page (for example in the locker box).
- Built-in sounds are generated live, so nothing is downloaded.
- For real recordings, download single key-press clips and load them with
  "Your own sound files" (up to 12 files, under 2 MB each). Good free sources:
  - OpenGameArt "Keyboard Soundpack #1" (CC0, 32 single key presses)
  - Freesound, filtering for the CC0 licence
  - Mixkit keyboard sounds (Mixkit licence)
  Check each file's licence before sharing the extension with others.
